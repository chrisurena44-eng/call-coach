        Setup with RingCentral
        1. Make your calls from the RingCentral web app at app.ringcentral.com in Chrome. This is the easiest setup.
        2. Wear a headset so your mic only picks up you.
        3. Open Call Coach in its own Chrome window beside RingCentral.
        4. Click Start listening. Chrome asks what to share: pick the RingCentral tab and turn on Also share tab audio.
        5. Using the RingCentral desktop app on Windows instead? Choose Entire screen and turn on Also share system audio.
        6. On a Mac, use app.ringcentral.com in Chrome. Chrome can't reliably hear the desktop app there.
        7. Watch the Caller audio bar at the top. It moves when the caller talks.
